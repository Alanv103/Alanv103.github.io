Five traps

1 · One-sided vs two-sided"higher / greater / more" = one-sided → Prob > t or Prob < t. "a difference / differ" = two-sided → Prob > |t|. The right one-sided p is under 0.5.

2 · Forgetting CrossThree effects in the box, and Model DF matches the formula. Otherwise your interaction never got tested.

3 · Reading main effects over a significant interactionInteraction row first, every time.

4 · Wrong layout or modeling typeFit Y by X = stacked. Matched Pairs = side by side. X must be Nominal.

5 · "All means are different"H₁ for ANOVA is at least one mean differs.
