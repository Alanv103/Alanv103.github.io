The math behind those numbers

One meanz = (x̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n) t = (x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n) df=n−1 CI x̄ ± t(α/2,n−1)·s/√n CI x̄ ± zα/₂·σ/√n (σ known)
Power1 x̄c = μ₀ ∓ zα·σ/√n 2 z = (x̄c−μtrue)/(σ/√n) 3 β = P(fail to reject|μtrue) 4 Power = 1 − β
Proportionsp̂ = (x₁+x₂)/(n₁+n₂) pooled z = (p̂₁−p̂₂)/√[p̂q̂(1/n₁+1/n₂)] CI (p̂₁−p̂₂) ± zα/₂√[p̂₁q̂₁/n₁+p̂₂q̂₂/n₂] paired CI (b−c)/n ± zα/₂√[⋯/n³]

Traps that cost points

  • Prob>|t| is two-sided. Halve for one-tailed H₁ only if the sign agrees; else p > .5.
  • Mean vs. Indiv. A singular noun ("a truck", "a school") wants the wider Indiv.
  • Blank Y. A row added for a prediction needs an empty Y or it moves the line.
  • Freq box. A count table without Count in Freq is read as 4 observations.
  • Paired ≠ two samples. Same people twice → drop ties, binomial test.
  • Center Polynomials. Uncheck it before reading raw quadratic coefficients.
  • "Holding all others constant" in every multiple-regression interpretation.
  • Units. x in thousands: 34,000 is x = 34, not 34000.
  • df: n−1 one mean · n−2 simple slope · n−k−1 multiple coefficient.
  • Never "accept H₀", never claim causation, never put x̄ in a hypothesis.
  • Row %. p̂ divides by that group's own row total.
  • R² can't compare models of different sizes — use RSquare Adj.

The math behind those numbers — regression

Least squaresSxx = Σ(x−x̄)² Syy = Σ(y−ȳ)² Sxy = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) b₁ = Sxy/Sxx b₀ = ȳ − b₁x̄ r = Sxy/√(Sxx·Syy) R² = r²
Tests & dfslope t = b₁/SE df = n−2 vs. value c t = (b₁−c)/SE df = n−2 coefficient t = bₖ/SE df = n−k−1 overall F df = k and n−k−1
Intervals & R²adjslope b₁ ± t(α/2, n−2)·SE(b₁) coef bₖ ± t(α/2, n−k−1)·SE(bₖ) R²adj = 1 − (1−R²)(n−1)/(n−k−1) quadratic ŷ = b₀ + b₁x + b₂x²
